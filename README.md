# Pasadena Attic Mold — Risk Checklist

Attic mold risk checklist for Pasadena homes.

A quick companion for pasadenaatticmoldremoval.com. Check your attic against common mold risk signs and know when to arrange a professional inspection. Guidance only — do not disturb suspected mold yourself.
