# Mold Removal vs. Mold Remediation: What Pasadena Homeowners Are Actually Paying For

The two phrases sound interchangeable, and the difference is where the money goes. Removal is only the cleaning part — getting growth off decking, rafters and insulation. Remediation is the whole job: containment so spores do not spread through the house, removal, fixing the moisture source that caused the growth, and a documented clearance check at the end. A quote for "removal" alone, with no containment and no source fix, is how the same attic ends up treated twice.

It also helps to know that California has no state mold license — anyone can print "certified" on a business card. Judge by the process instead: a real inspection with moisture readings and photos, an itemized written scope, containment and negative air during the work, and the moisture source fixed, not just fogged. For scale, most residential attic jobs in the Pasadena area land between $1,500 and $4,000, priced on the affected area — not the size of the whole attic.
