# Attic Mold or Just Mildew? How to Tell Them Apart in a Pasadena Attic

Plenty of Pasadena homeowners climb into the attic, see grey-white fuzz on the rafters, and hear two different answers depending on who they ask: "just mildew, wipe it off" or "full remediation." The two growths are related, but they behave differently, live in different places, and call for different responses. Here's how to read what you're looking at.

## Mildew: the surface tenant

Mildew is a surface growth. It sits on top of damp material as a flat, powdery film — usually white, grey or yellowish — and it's common on painted surfaces, cardboard storage boxes and anything that stays humid without getting truly wet. It smells musty but mild. Wiped with a cloth, it comes away easily, because it never went deeper than the surface it landed on.

## Mold: the one that moves in

Mold grows *into* porous material, not just on it. In an attic that means the roof decking, rafters and insulation. It tends to look patchy and textured rather than powdery — black, green, brown or white clusters that spread outward from a moisture source: a slow roof leak, a bathroom fan venting into the attic instead of outside, condensation on cold decking in winter. The smell is stronger and earthier, and it lingers in the rooms below. Scrubbing the visible surface doesn't finish the job, because the growth runs into the wood itself.

## The attic clues that settle it

- **Location.** Growth directly under a roof stain, around a vent pipe, or above the bathroom points to a moisture source feeding it — that's mold behaviour, and the source has to be fixed or it returns.
- **Texture.** Powdery and flat, wipes clean: likely mildew. Fuzzy, raised, or staining the wood grain: mold.
- **Spread.** Mildew stays where the damp surface is. Mold colonies expand between visits. Photograph the patch with a coin for scale and check it in a few weeks — growth that has moved is telling you something.

## Why the distinction is worth money

Treating mildew like mold means paying remediation prices for a cleaning job. Treating mold like mildew means a cosmetic wipe-down, a returning smell, and a bigger colony next season. The honest answer comes from looking at the moisture source as much as the growth — in attics, the water is always the real problem.
