# Signs Your Pasadena Attic Has Mold — and When Removal Is Actually Needed

Attic mold is almost always silent until it isn't. Most Pasadena homeowners find it by accident — a smell, a stain, or a home inspector's flashlight during a sale. Here are the signs worth acting on.

**The smell test.** A musty odor in upstairs rooms or near the attic hatch is usually the first signal. If it is stronger after rain or in humid spring weather, moisture is feeding something up there.

**What you can see from the hatch.** Dark staining on the underside of the roof deck or along the rafters is the clearest visual sign. A ceiling stain that keeps coming back after painting points the same way. And for under-ventilated attics there is a simple self-check: if you cannot see daylight through the soffit vents from inside the attic, they are likely blocked by insulation or were never properly cut — trapped moist air is the mechanism behind most attic mold.

**Reasons to get it checked even without signs.** A recent roof leak. A bathroom or dryer fan that vents into the attic instead of outside. Or a pre-1960s home you are preparing to sell — many of Pasadena's Craftsman bungalows were framed before modern ventilation codes, and California law requires sellers to disclose known mold on the Transfer Disclosure Statement.

**When removal is needed.** Cleaning alone fails when the moisture source stays — the blocked vent, the mis-routed fan, the leak. Real remediation seals the area with HEPA filtration and negative air pressure, removes what cannot be cleaned, treats what can, fixes the cause, and confirms with a clearance sample.
