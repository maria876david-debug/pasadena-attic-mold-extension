# Attic Ventilation and Mould in Pasadena: The Link Most Homes Miss

Most attic mould starts with ventilation, not with a roof leak. When warm, moist air from bathrooms and kitchens is vented into the attic instead of outside, or when vents are blocked by insulation, moisture collects on the underside of the roof and stays there. Over time that damp, dark space grows mould on the wood and insulation.

Signs to look for: a musty smell when you open the attic hatch, dark staining on the roof decking, damp insulation, or frost on the underside of the roof in cold weather. Fixing the ventilation - clear vents, proper exhausts to the outside - stops the moisture at its source. Cleaning the mould without fixing the air flow just means cleaning it again next year.
