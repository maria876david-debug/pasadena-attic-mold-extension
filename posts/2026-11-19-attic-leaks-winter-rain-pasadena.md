# Attic Leaks in Winter Rain: Where Pasadena Roofs Let Water In

Pasadena rain is rare, which is exactly why it catches attics out. Months of dry heat dry out the sealant around vents, flashing and chimney joints, and the first winter rain finds every gap the summer opened. The water rarely comes through the middle of the roof - it comes in at the edges: flashing that has pulled away from a chimney, a vent boot cracked by the sun, or a valley where leaves have dammed the water and pushed it under the shingles. From inside the attic, the signs are dark stains on the decking, damp insulation, and a musty smell that gets stronger after rain. Before the rainy season, check the flashing and vent boots from the ground with binoculars, and have anything cracked or lifted resealed. A leak found before the rain is a small repair; a leak found by the stain on the ceiling is a restoration job.
