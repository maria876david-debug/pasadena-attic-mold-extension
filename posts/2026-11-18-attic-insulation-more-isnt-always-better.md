# Attic Insulation in Pasadena: Why More Isn't Always Better

Adding more insulation to a Pasadena attic sounds like an easy win - until the extra layer blocks the vents. Insulation works by trapping air, but an attic also needs to breathe: soffit and ridge vents move moist air out, and insulation piled over those vents traps the moisture inside. The result is the opposite of what was intended - damp insulation, a musty smell, and mould on the roof decking. Before adding insulation, check three things: that the vents are clear, that bathroom and kitchen fans exhaust outside rather than into the attic, and that the existing insulation is dry. Fix the airflow first, then add insulation if the level is genuinely low.
